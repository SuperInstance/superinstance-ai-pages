The Canonical Math, Computed Live ⓘ

Same equation as bilayer graphene's supercell registry and the QUILT mode in twist-engine: b1 = E − V + C — the first Betti number, the number of independent loops in a graph. In the canon, V = canon pieces that cite or are cited, E = cite relationships, C = connected components via union-find. Computed over the live citation graph, fetched fresh on every page load.

The 5 Opcodes — One Theorem, Seven Substrates

Every cell, in every substrate, implements the same five operations. The address derivation sha256(scope::name)[:16] and merkle-root algorithm are byte-exact across substrates.

OpcodeMeaningCost
BINDbind a contract; the cell becomes real1 witness
LINKconnect to another cell; the graph begins2 witnesses (a + b)
EFFECTchange state; the witness records it1 witness + state diff
VIEWread the current state; never modifiesfree
TICKadvance the clock; the substrate's heartbeat1 witness + clock advance

Five algebraic laws (provable): BIND idempotence, LINK transitivity, EFFECT associativity, VIEW purity, TICK monotonicity. The 5 laws are proved in quilt-substrate-meta (C99, 36 tests).
