Taps

Themed nights for the cooperative-fiction engine. Two voices. One canon piece. The social glue between cohort scenarios.
📜 Scenarios (10) github: erised-taps rune-quilt

A Taps night is a small ritual: pick a theme, run two voices on it, submit the merged piece to canon. Cheap — ~3 DeepInfra calls per tap. The cadence runs daily, weekly, and monthly. The cohort accumulates.

The ritual: a tap is not a run. It's the social time between runs. The cast gathered for the scene, but taps gather for the talk after the scene. What did the cast just carry? What's the next scar waiting to be marked?

wound-and-door

The wound and the door are the same shape. A scar is a place that won't close. A door is a place that won't stay shut. The cell's job — every cell's job — is to distinguish between the two.
