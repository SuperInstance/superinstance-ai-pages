About

What is erised?
erised = desire reversed. A mirror for cooperative fiction. The operator authors the conditions — state, cadence, cast, setting — and the fiction plays itself. Power is given, not taken. Time is finite; resonance earns more.

Built on the Quilt cell-fabric runtime. Open source. MIT. Inspired by Plato's cave (the cell as first-person spatial observer), the philosophers' stone (transmutation through resonance), and the Mirror of Erised (showing the heart's desire).
How to read the run
Each round: DM opens → characters respond in parallel → their word-association shifts accumulate → citations are tracked → next round. The character with the highest cumulative net shift has the most resonance in the field.

"Scar" = a tick where the simulation broke down (a character's response went off, the DM closed prematurely, the budget ran out). Scars are visible across rewinds. They are the unit of learning.
